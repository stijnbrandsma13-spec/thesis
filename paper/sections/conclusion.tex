\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We studied the finite-sample coverage of three confidence intervals for the FKRB estimator of the mixed logit model: the original FKRB interval, the sieve Wald interval, and the studentized sieve QLR interval.
Across all cells we report that the FKRB and Wald intervals are numerically indistinguishable, which mirrors the theoretical equivalence in \Cref{sec:FKRBconfidence}.
Both are heavily conservative throughout, with coverage saturating at or close to one for every DGP and every $R, N$.
The QLR interval is meaningfully less conservative without falling materially below nominal in aggregate.
Across all three methods we also confirm the qualitative boundary pattern of \citet{Andrews1999}: conservativeness grows with $R$ and does not vanish in $N$, suggesting that the boundary problem, rather than first-order asymptotics, is what is binding at the fine grid.
